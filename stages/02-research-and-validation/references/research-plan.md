# Research Plan

## Required Research Areas

- Market context
- Demographic context
- Problem validity
- Persona signals
- Competitive landscape
- Representative participant sample criteria
- Interview recruitment rationale
- Open-ended, clear, neutral, conversational interview questions
- User research plan or synthetic participant profiles

## Source Guidance

Use current sources when available. If internet access is unavailable, state the limitation and produce a research plan plus hypothesis-based synthesis.

## Output Sections

- Research questions
- Findings
- Evidence table
- Assumption log
- Competitive landscape notes
- Interview plan
- Participant sample rationale
- Implications for empathize artifacts
