# Stage 06: Case Study Assembly

Assemble all prior work into a portfolio-ready UX case study that reads as a design thinking story, with every prior-stage artifact woven into its phase.

## Process

1. Read all previous stage outputs.
2. Structure the case study by phase: Problem, Research, Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype Direction, Validation / Testing Plan, and Outcome / Limits / Next Steps.
3. Weave every artifact from stages 01-05 into the phase where it belongs, giving each artifact its own `###` subheader: brief, evidence, assumptions, plans, personas, stories, journeys, maps, needs, hypotheses, HMWs, concepts, feature decisions, flows, requirements, wireframes, and audits.
4. Build the evidence chain from problem to research signal to human insight to definition to ideation decision.
5. Preserve source traceability with short source notes where useful.
6. Include research limitations and next steps.
7. Verify every prior-stage artifact appears under its own subheader in its phase and has a purpose or consequence.
8. Run the audit, revise if needed, then save to output/.

## Audit

| Check | Pass Condition |
|-------|---------------|
| Evidence chain | Major decisions trace to research or assumptions |
| Phase completeness | Every artifact from stages 01-05 appears under its own subheader inside the relevant phase |
| Phase consequence | Each artifact explains what decision, insight, or risk it shaped |
| Portfolio readiness | Case study is credible, concise, and reviewable |
| Limits named | Research gaps and next steps are explicit |
