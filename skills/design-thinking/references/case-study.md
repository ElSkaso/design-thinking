# UX Case Study Reference

Use this reference to turn design thinking artifacts into a portfolio-ready case study that reads like the story of the design thinking process.

## Case Study Structure

Recommended sections:

1. Project overview
2. Problem
3. Research
4. Empathize
5. Define
6. Ideate
7. Prototype direction
8. Validation / testing plan
9. Outcome, limits, and next steps

## Evidence Chain

For every major design decision, show:

```text
Research signal -> insight -> artifact -> design decision -> expected user outcome
```

## Phase Narrative

Each phase should read as a coherent part of the project story, not as a separate audit document. Include every generated artifact from the prior stage outputs inside the phase where it belongs, and give each artifact its own `###` subheader.

- Problem: project brief, raw problem, target users, constraints, known facts, assumptions, open questions, desired outcomes, research questions, success signals, and audit.
- Research: research purpose, inputs, questions, findings, evidence, assumptions, competitive notes, validation synthesis, research plan, implications, limits, and audit.
- Empathize: evidence basis, interview plan, hypothesis-based summaries, personas, user stories, journey maps, empathy maps, aggregated empathy map, accessibility notes, edge cases, and audit.
- Define: inputs, core frame, 5W/H, problem statements, user needs, insights, hypotheses, problem/hypothesis pairs, value proposition, workarounds, human factors rationale, risks, guiding definition, and audit.
- Ideate: inputs, guiding problem, competitive audit, goals, HMWs, sketches, selection rationale, feature prioritization, MVP direction, user flow, screen requirements, text wireframes, interaction requirements, accessibility requirements, design direction, app scope, readiness notes, and audit.

Use source notes where helpful, but keep artifacts inside the phase narrative where they advance the story.

## Portfolio Quality Bar

A strong case study:

- Shows why the problem matters.
- Names research limits honestly.
- Uses every concrete artifact as part of the phase story.
- Explains tradeoffs and rejected options.
- Connects UX decisions to user evidence.
- Shows the product outcome clearly.
- Includes next steps or validation plan.

Avoid:

- Treating design thinking phases as a checklist without decisions.
- Personas with no impact on the product.
- Competitive audits that only describe visuals.
- Problem statements that secretly prescribe the solution.
- Polished screens with no evidence trail.

## Case Study Audit

- The narrative can be followed from vague problem to working concept.
- Each artifact has a purpose and consequence.
- Every artifact from prior stage outputs appears under its own subheader in its design thinking phase.
- Research limits are explicit.
- The final app/prototype reflects the defined problem.
- The case study is credible to UX reviewers and understandable to non-designers.
