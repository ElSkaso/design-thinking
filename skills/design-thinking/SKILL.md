# Design Thinking

Use this skill when the task involves UX design thinking, portfolio case studies, product problem discovery, or turning research into app/prototype direction.

## Core Workflow

1. Frame the challenge.
   - Start from the user's rough problem, idea, brief, audience, and constraints.
   - Separate facts, assumptions, research questions, and desired outcomes.
   - Do not lock a solution before research synthesis.

2. Empathize.
   - Gather or simulate structured interview inputs from representative participants or clearly labeled synthetic profiles.
   - Conduct or synthesize interviews before creating artifacts.
   - Distill each interview into an empathy map, group users by shared characteristics, goals, or needs, then create personas, one-sentence user stories, journey maps, and accessibility considerations.
   - Load `references/empathize.md` for artifact formats and quality checks.

3. Define.
   - Convert research into a clear problem frame.
   - Produce problem statements, user needs statements, hypothesis statements, problem/hypothesis pairs, and value propositions.
   - Load `references/define.md` for formulas and review criteria.

4. Ideate.
   - Generate solution directions from the defined problem, not from taste alone.
   - Produce HMW questions, goal statements, competitive audits, rapid sketch prompts, feature candidates, and wireframe requirements.
   - Load `references/ideate.md` for methods and artifact templates.

5. Prototype and test.
   - Translate selected ideas into prototype scope, screen flows, interaction requirements, and test tasks.
   - Load `references/prototype-test.md` when the work continues into prototype planning or usability testing.

## Evidence Rules

- Label unsupported claims as assumptions.
- Tie each empathy map, persona, pain point, feature, and design decision to interview evidence, a research signal, or an explicit constraint.
- Derive personas from user groups after empathy-map synthesis, not directly from desk research.
- Preserve contradictions in research instead of smoothing them away too early.
- Prefer realistic user language over generic UX phrasing.
- For portfolio case studies, show the reasoning chain: problem -> research signal -> insight -> artifact -> design decision.

## Artifact Standards

Every artifact should include:

- Purpose: why this artifact exists in the project.
- Inputs: which research notes, assumptions, or prior artifacts informed it.
- Output: the artifact itself.
- Design implication: what changes because of this artifact.
- Confidence: high, medium, or low, based on evidence quality.

## Human Checkpoints

Pause for review after:

- Research synthesis, before defining the problem.
- Problem/hypothesis framing, before ideation.
- Wireframes or prototype scope, before app generation.

Ask the human to correct missing users, weak evidence, unrealistic assumptions, or solution directions that do not fit the project.
