# Evidence Standards

## Evidence Labels

- Evidence: supported by research, user input, competitor observation, or supplied material.
- Assumption: plausible but not validated.
- Risk: a decision that could fail without further validation.
- Open question: an unknown that should shape later research or testing.

## Confidence Levels

- High: multiple strong signals support the claim.
- Medium: at least one credible signal supports the claim.
- Low: the claim is mostly inferred and needs validation.

## Rules

- Do not hide uncertainty.
- Tie design decisions to research signals.
- Preserve contradictions in the evidence.
- Use user language when available.
